CodeSprint 2026 was a 32-hour national-level hackathon at St Aloysius (Deemed to be University), Mangaluru. The problem statement was wide open: **build something that helps students learn.**

My teammate [@whyismeleige](https://github.com/whyismeleige) and I built **TradeQuest**, a gamified virtual stock trading league for students. Competing as Team ByteMonks, we won the external participants category.

## The idea

Trading is a skill students are curious about but have no safe way to practise. TradeQuest gives them a market they can't lose real money in, and makes it competitive enough that they keep coming back:

- **Real-time price updates**, so the market feels alive.
- **Daily, weekly and monthly leagues** with live leaderboards.
- **Achievements and XP** to reward progress.

## The technical bet: our own market

We decided not to depend on live market data. Instead, we wrote a **simulation-based algorithm** from scratch to drive how prices move. That gave us full control over the market and no dependency on an outside data feed during a live demo. It was also the hardest part of the build: under heavy time pressure, the market still had to feel realistic.

The stack was Node.js and Next.js for the app, FastAPI for backend services, AWS for hosting and Cloudinary for media.

## Working in 32 hours

With two people and a fixed deadline, the real constraint was coordination. We made architectural decisions fast and split the work carefully so neither of us was ever waiting on the other.

## The result

We shipped a complete, working, demoable product inside the 32-hour window and took **first place in the external participants category**. [Daijiworld covered the event](/press).
