## Overview
TradeQuest is a gamified virtual stock trading platform built for students, created with a teammate during CodeSprint 2026, a 32-hour national-level hackathon hosted by St Aloysius (Deemed to be University), Mangaluru. We took an open-ended problem statement — build something that helps students learn — and shipped a full working product in the allotted time. As Team ByteMonks, we won the external participants category.
## Key Features
- **Real-Time Price Updates**: Live pricing pushed to users so the market feels alive during trading sessions.
- **Competitive Leagues**: Daily, weekly, and monthly leagues with live leaderboards.
- **Custom Simulation Algorithm**: Built our own simulation-based algorithm to drive realistic market behavior rather than relying on live market data feeds.
- **Gamification Layer**: Achievement unlocks and XP progression to keep students engaged with learning to trade.
## Technologies Used
- **Node.js and Next.js**: For the application.
- **FastAPI**: For backend services.
- **AWS**: For hosting and infrastructure.
- **Cloudinary**: For media handling.
## Challenges and Learnings
The biggest constraint was time — 32 hours to go from an open problem statement to a working, demoable product. We had to make fast architectural decisions and split work carefully between the two of us to avoid blocking each other. Building a simulation algorithm that felt realistic without live market data, under that time pressure, was the core technical challenge.
## Outcome
We delivered a complete, working product within the 32-hour window and, as Team ByteMonks, won first place in the external participants category — covered by [Daijiworld](/press).
