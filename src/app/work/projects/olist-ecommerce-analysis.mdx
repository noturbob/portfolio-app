## Overview
A full analytics pipeline over the Olist Brazilian e-commerce dataset — 99,441 orders, 3,095 sellers and 32,951 products across 27 states, from Sep 2016 to Oct 2018.
## Key Features
- **Data cleaning**: 668K records across 8 datasets, with derived features for RFM analysis and delivery metrics.
- **SQL analytics**: 9 business queries covering geography, payment methods, category performance, delivery performance and seller analytics.
- **Visualizations**: 14+ interactive Plotly charts and a Power BI dashboard.
- **Machine learning**: A Random Forest delivery-delay classifier reaching 92.16% accuracy.
## Technologies Used
- **Python, pandas, scikit-learn, SQL, Plotly, Power BI**.
