## Overview
A registration platform built for a Mini Militia clan's MMC 2026 tournament — 48 player slots, a full bracket format, wildcard entries and livestreamed matches. It handles player sign-ups, lays out the rules, and routes submissions straight into Google Sheets.
## Key Features
- **Landing page**: Tournament overview, rules, titles on the line, match flow and streaming policy.
- **Registration form**: Player name, Discord ID, WhatsApp number and an avatar screenshot.
- **Server-side handling**: Avatars go to Cloudinary; each entry is logged to a Google Sheet through a service account.
- **Custom "beveled" component system** for buttons, cards and inputs.
## Technologies Used
- **Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4** on Vercel.
- **Node.js, Express, Multer, Cloudinary, Google Sheets API** on Render.
