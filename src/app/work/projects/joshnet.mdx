## Overview
JoshNet is a campus ecosystem mobile app built as the final-year project for the BBA IT program at St. Joseph's Degree & PG College, Hyderabad. Campus systems are usually fragmented across separate apps for attendance, materials, communication and academic tracking; JoshNet unifies them in one role-aware platform for students, faculty, alumni and administrators.
## Key Features
- **Attendance dashboard**: Visual heatmaps with a 75% eligibility calculator and predictions.
- **Marks management**: Internal assessment entry with auto-calculation.
- **Real-time communication**: Department chat rooms over WebSockets.
- **AI assistant** for instant help.
- **Security**: Two-factor authentication, session management across devices and account lockout protection.
## Technologies Used
- **React Native (Expo), TypeScript, NativeWind**: Mobile app.
- **Node.js, Express 5, MongoDB, Mongoose, Socket.IO, Nodemailer**: Backend.
